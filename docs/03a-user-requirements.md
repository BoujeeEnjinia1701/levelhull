# LevelHull user requirements

What fishers, owners, builders and safety programmes need, written from their side. Product requirements that meet these needs are in LVH-PRS-001 (`03-requirements.md`) and the links are in the traceability matrix (`03b-traceability.md`). All are proposals for review with the first co-design partner. Method: STANDARDS section 22.

> **Safety:** LevelHull is flotation for the boat, never a substitute for life jackets. UR-015 makes that a requirement in its own right.

Table 1. User requirements.

| ID | Category | Title | Need | Priority | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| UR-001 | Performance | Stays afloat when swamped | When a wave fills our canoe, it stays afloat with us holding on, the motor, the nets and the catch, long enough to be bailed or rescued | High | Canoe fishers and crews (PRB Table 1a) |
| UR-002 | Performance | Gunwale clear of the water | The swamped canoe floats with its gunwale out of the water so we can bail it rather than watch the waves refill it | High | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-003 | Safety | Floats upright and level | The swamped canoe floats upright and level and does not roll over on us | High | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-004 | Usability | Can be bailed out | Two of us can bail it out with what is aboard and get going again | High | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-005 | Usability | Room to work | It leaves room to work, stow the catch and handle the nets | High | Canoe owners |
| UR-006 | Environment and durability | Keeps its lift | It keeps its lift in water, strong sun, fuel and fish slime | High | Canoe owners |
| UR-007 | Safety | Stays fixed | It stays fixed to the canoe when the canoe is full of water, struck by waves or on its side | High | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-008 | Deployment and assembly | Fitted locally in a day | A local builder can fit it in a working day with hand tools, without changing the hull shape and without new holes below the waterline | High | Local boatbuilders |
| UR-009 | Environment and durability | Lasts | It lasts at least a year of normal use without cracking, tearing or losing lift | Medium | Canoe owners |
| UR-010 | Cost | Affordable | The kit costs what an owner or a safety programme can pay | Medium | Canoe owners; fisheries departments and NGOs |
| UR-011 | Performance | Reserve | It still copes with an extra person holding on or a wet net more than planned | Medium | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-012 | Safety | Damage tolerant | If one piece is torn or lost the canoe still floats | Medium | Fisheries departments, BMUs and NGOs |
| UR-013 | Safety | Crew on one side | It stays upright when all of us grab the same side | High | Canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-014 | Usability | Out of the way | It does not hinder beaching, landing alongside other canoes, paddling, motoring or hauling nets over the side | Medium | Canoe owners; canoe fishers and crews |
| UR-015 | Regulatory | With life jackets, never instead | The kit and everything said about it make clear it is not a life jacket and never replaces one | High | Fisheries departments, BMUs and NGOs |
| UR-016 | Logistics | Sizes for other canoes | Builders can size it for the canoes they have, of other lengths, beams and motors | Medium | Local boatbuilders; fisheries research institutes |

Categories: deployment and assembly, performance, usability, safety, environment and durability, logistics, maintenance, cost, regulatory. Priorities: High, Medium, Low.
