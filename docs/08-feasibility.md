# LevelHull feasibility assessment

LevelHull is feasible on paper: the physics holds with margin once the water inside is down to the lake level (LVH-STM-001, LVH-CAL-001), with the first minutes after swamping marginal until bailed (D-O2, D-O3 with Amish), every part can be made by a beach yard and a tailor with hand tools, and every material is sold in East Africa; the weak points are not technical but the life of the covers and straps in the sun, the lost space the owners must accept and an unknown approval route (LVH-REG-001 G1). The cost is over the value-engineering target by USD 360 for two canoes. Method: STANDARDS section 23.

> **Safety:** Feasible on paper does not mean safe to use. No fitted canoe goes fishing before the pull test and a swamp test in sheltered, shallow water with a safety boat and everyone in a life jacket (LVH-BLD-001, sections 5 and 6).
